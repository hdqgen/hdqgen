## 🧠 What is HDQGen?

**HDQGen** is an all in one local AI studio for desktop. It brings together **chat, image generation, video generation, and a coding agent** in a single app, all running **privately on your machine**, with **no cloud dependency** and **no subscription**.

Everything you'd normally stitch together from separate apps, servers, and API keys is built into HDQGen from the start. Download local models, pick the ones you want, and start generating. Your chats, images, videos, and code never leave your device.

> 🔗 Full details, demos, and testimonials: **[hdqgen.com](https://hdqgen.com/)**

## ✨ Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 💬 **Chat** | Talk to open local language models for drafting, research, and reasoning. Every conversation stays on your machine. |
| 🖼️ **Image Generation** | Generate images with local diffusion models. Swap models freely and iterate on prompts privately. |
| 🎬 **Video Generation** | Produce short video clips (text to video and image to video) directly on device. No render queues, no cloud credits. |
| 🤖 **Coding Agent** | Hand off small tasks to a local coding agent that can read, edit, and run code in your project without your source ever leaving your desktop. |

### Why local?

* 🔒 **Privacy.** Your data stays with you. No cloud servers, no uploads, no third party storing your data.
* 💰 **Pay once, use freely.** Buy once and use open, free models with no per message charges or monthly fees.
* ⚡ **Built for your machine.** Use your GPU for speed, or run entirely on CPU. Your machine, your models, your choice.

## ❓ FAQ

**Does HDQGen require an internet connection?**
No. Once installed and your models are downloaded, HDQGen runs fully offline.

**Do I need a GPU?**
No, HDQGen runs on CPU as well, though a GPU will speed up generation significantly.

**Is this a subscription?**
No. HDQGen is a one time purchase of $29.99 for lifetime use.

**Which models can I use?**
HDQGen works with free, open local models for chat, image, and video generation, which you download and manage from within the app.
